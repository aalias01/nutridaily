Your nutrition tracker

Log common foods (banana, eggs, rice…) from the built-in list. No AI needed for those.

Homemade dishes: describe them to an LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.), paste the answer here, then log by grams.

Tracking works fully without signing in. Google Sign-in is optional and only for syncing to your Drive so a cleared browser or second device can restore your log.

Settings

Mode

Meals save on this device automatically. Sign-in is optional: it keeps a copy in your Google Drive so another phone or a cleared browser cache can restore it. Nothing is sent anywhere except your own Drive.

After reopening the app (especially on iPhone), Drive may pause until you tap Reconnect on the banner or the sync pill. Logging still works on this device meanwhile.

Advanced: override Client ID
On the live deploy, a Client ID is injected at build time. Paste your own here to override (forks / local). Leave empty to use the deploy default.

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, or any LLM that returns a NUTRI v1 block.

This browser holds the working copy. Export a JSON backup anytime. Clearing site data (or uninstalling) deletes the local copy unless you signed in with Google or exported first.

About NutriDaily

Personal nutrition tracker. Common foods use USDA-style reference averages; homemade dishes come from an AI paste you review. Log by grams, keep an event ledger on device. Optional Google Drive sync. Open source on GitHub. Live: nutridaily.vercel.app.
